# - Idea Origin

## How the Idea Started

The idea for CampusFix came from a common problem observed in
college and hostel environments.

Students often face issues such as Wi-Fi problems, water supply,
electricity, cleanliness, classroom maintenance, and other campus
facilities.

We noticed that when multiple students face the same problem,
they may report it separately. This can create duplicate complaints
and makes it difficult for management to understand how many
students are actually affected.

This led us to think of a simpler approach:

Instead of every student creating a separate complaint, a student
can find an existing issue and select:

"I'm facing this too"

The issue then shows the number of students affected by it.

## Why We Chose This Approach

We wanted to build a solution that is simple for students and useful
for campus management.

Our idea focuses on three things:

- Reducing duplicate complaints
- Showing the real impact of a problem
- Helping management prioritize important issues

We also included Campus and Hostel as separate problem areas so
that issues can be reported in the correct context.

## Our Key Idea

The main idea behind CampusFix is:

"One issue, multiple confirmations — instead of multiple duplicate
complaints."

This turns individual student complaints into a clearer picture of
the problems affecting the campus community.
